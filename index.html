Galaxy Racer does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, files, microphone recordings, or payment information.

Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or receive information needed to provide, measure, personalize where permitted, secure, and improve advertising. 2. How information is used

Advertising-related information is used to show and measure ads, prevent invalid activity and fraud, maintain the advertising service, comply with legal obligations, and, where permitted and based on applicable settings or consent, personalize advertising.

3. Third-party services

The Game uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to serve interstitial ads. Google’s handling of information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and its information about how Google uses data when you use sites or apps that use Google services. You can manage advertising preferences through your Android device settings and any consent or privacy-options control made available in the Game.

4. Your privacy choices

Where required by law, we will request consent before requesting ads that require consent. Where a privacy-options entry point is required, the Game will make it available so you can review or change your choices. You may also reset or limit your device advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads where available, through Android settings. These settings do not necessarily stop all ads; they may result in less relevant ads.

5. Data retention and security

We do not operate an account database for Galaxy Racer. Advertising-related data is processed and retained by Google and its partners according to their applicable policies and legal obligations. We rely on reputable service providers and reasonable technical safeguards, but no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Children’s privacy

Galaxy Racer is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in the user’s country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at the address below. If the Game is later made available to children or enrolled in Google Play’s Families program, we will configure advertising and data practices accordingly and update this policy before that release.

7. International processing

Google and its service providers may process advertising-related information in countries other than the country where you live. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. Google’s applicable safeguards and policies govern this processing.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game or applicable requirements change. We will post the revised version at the policy URL and update the “Last updated” date. Material changes will be handled as required by applicable law.
